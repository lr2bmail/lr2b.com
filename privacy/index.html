Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

What we receive

When you email LR2B, directly or through the form on the home page (which only opens your own email app), we receive what you choose to send: your name, email address, project details, links and files. Our host, Cloudflare, processes basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the website.

How we use it

We use it to reply to you, discuss and deliver projects, prevent abuse and keep basic business records. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from lr2b.com itself.

Our products and other sites

Links lead to our products and to third-party sites such as Google Play and the App Store. Each of our products has its own privacy policy on its own website, and third-party sites follow their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep project and contact information only as long as needed for communication, delivery, records, security or legal requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@lr2b.com.
